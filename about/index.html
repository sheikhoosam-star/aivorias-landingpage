AIvorias is an AI-powered technology platform delivering complete AI and automation solutions for businesses across every industry: healthcare, retail, real estate, and beyond.

Where we started

AIvorias began with doctors and clinics, where a missed call is a patient who did not get care. It is a demanding place to build. The agent has to be accurate, calm and quick, in two languages, every time.

Everything we now offer to other industries is tested against that standard. A voice agent that can handle a worried patient at midnight can handle a property enquiry or an order query.
